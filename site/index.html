✈️ Boston → Istanbul → Dhaka → Bangkok + Singapore

Trip handbook — tap a section

📋 Book in this order
  1. Passports first — all 3 (child too) valid 6+ months past Feb 2027 (into Aug 2027) with 2+ blank pages each.
  2. Ticket ① (Boston→Istanbul→Dhaka + the return from whichever city comes last) on the airline's US website/app. Booking there starts the federal 24-hour free-cancel window and keeps refunds simple. Avoid third-party sites.
  3. Same day: Bangkok Marriott on points (5 nights, 5th free). Points rates usually cancel free until a few days out. If the hotel doesn't work, cancel the flight within 24h at $0.
  4. Bangladesh visas — see Visas below. Do this before buying Ticket ②.
  5. Ticket ② last (the Dhaka→Bangkok/Singapore middle, in whichever order won). It never touches the US so the 24-hour rule does not apply. Treat it as non-refundable and buy it only once everything else is locked.
  6. ≤3 days before landing: Thailand's TDAC digital arrival card + Singapore's SG Arrival Card, both free and online; no visas needed on US passports.
🧳 Baggage — why it's all over the place
  • Two different rulebooks. Ticket ① starts in the USA, so it's counted in bags (piece concept). Turkish and the Gulf carriers give 2 × 23 kg per person, most European and US carriers give only 1. Ticket ② never touches the US, so it's counted in kilos, and the number changes by airline and by route.
  • Nothing is through-checked between the two tickets. You collect the bags in Dhaka and check them in again for Ticket ②; same again at the middle stop if the middle is two separate tickets.
  • Low-cost carriers (AirAsia, Scoot, Jetstar, Thai LCCs) include no free checked bag at all. The fare looks cheaper until you add 20 kg, and buying at the airport costs roughly double.
  • The child with a seat normally gets the full adult allowance. Infants on a lap do not.
  • The figures on the Trip tab come from each airline's own baggage page. Fare brands (EcoFly, Economy Lite, Basic) can cut them. The number on the checkout page is the one you actually bought.
💳 Cancellations & refunds
  • 24-hour rule (US DOT): any ticket that touches the US — Ticket ① — is fully refundable for 24 hours after booking if departure is 7+ days away. This is the main safety valve; Turkish honors it only when booked via its own site/app.
  • After 24h on Turkish: economy cancel fee roughly $70–200+ per person; the cheapest "EcoFly" promo fares are non-refundable. If unsure, pay the small step-up to a flexible economy fare. The exact rule shows at checkout. No cancellations in the last 12h before departure.
  • Ticket ② (the Dhaka→Bangkok/Singapore middle): no US protection. AirAsia/Scoot/Thai LCCs are effectively non-refundable; full-service carriers charge fare-rule fees. Assume this money is spent once booked.
  • Airline-initiated changes: if the airline significantly changes or cancels any flight, you're owed a full refund regardless of fare type.
  • Card refunds must arrive within 7 business days (DOT). Pay with a credit card that has trip protection.
  • Chase UR points: portal bookings get real Turkish (235-) ticket numbers, so the stopover hotel usually still works. N/R fare classes are excluded, and changes route through Chase. Cleanest: book the airline direct with the card, spend UR on Ticket ② instead.
🛂 Visas & borders
  • Bangladesh: US passports get a 30-day visa on arrival at Dhaka — $50 cash USD per person (child too; carry $150 crisp), onward ticket + proof of funds (~$500), and it's granted at the officer's discretion. Getting tourist visas in advance from the mission is the safer play. Your stay keeps a buffer: never let the departure slip past day 30.
  • Thailand: US passports enter visa-exempt (30+ days — a 5-night stay is well inside; no fee). File the free TDAC digital arrival card online ≤3 days before landing, kids included.
  • Istanbul stopover: US citizens are visa-free in Türkiye (90 days/180). Leaving the airport for the 2 nights is fine, no visa needed.
  • Singapore: visa-free for US passports; file the free SG Arrival Card online ≤3 days before landing.
  • Transit airside on a single ticket needs no visa. Only if a hop is booked as two separate tickets would you clear immigration in the connecting country.
🕌 The Istanbul stopover perk
  • Turkish gives economy passengers a FREE 4-star hotel night when the connection is 20 hr–7 days on one reservation: book Ticket ①, then apply at their Stopover "Booker" page ≥72 hr before the flight. Not valid on N/R fare classes. Check the booking-class letter before paying. On the 2-night version, night 2 is on you.
  • IST→DAC flies once daily, which is why the Istanbul stop costs so little extra. You'd be waiting for that flight anyway.
